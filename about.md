## Hello, I'm Abhi.

I'm a computer science student interested in how software works, how developers build it, and how the right tools can make the process better.

This site is where I document my journey—not just finished projects, but also experiments, discoveries, mistakes, and lessons along the way.

## What I'm exploring

I'm interested in several areas of software development:

- **Software engineering** — programming concepts, problem-solving, and better ways to design software.
- **Backend development** — Java, application architecture, APIs, and reliable applications.
- **Linux and developer tools** — exploring my development environment and tools that improve everyday workflows.
- **Open source** — exploring projects, reading code, learning from other developers, and building things of my own.

I'm not limiting myself to one technology. I enjoy trying different tools, comparing approaches, and understanding where each technology fits best.

## Why I write

Writing helps me understand what I learn and create a record I can revisit. When I solve a problem, discover an interesting tool, or experiment with a technology, I want to document it clearly enough that someone else might benefit too.

Not every experiment becomes a project, and learning is rarely a straight line. This blog reflects that process as it happens.
